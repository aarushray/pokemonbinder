Manage designs

Upload artwork, choose each binder's type, colour and price, then publish it to the shop.

Pages

Each page of the shop has its own subclasses, shown to customers as filters. Removing a page or subclass never deletes designs.

Storewide discount

Takes this percentage off every binder in the shop, on every page. Custom designs are never discounted. While it's on, binders' own discounts are reset to 0 and can't be edited. Set 0 to end the sale and allow binders' own discounts again.

While the discount is on, this text scrolls across a banner at the top of the home page. Leave it empty to show "20% off all binders".

Colour availability

For each binder size, click a colour to cross it out when it's out of stock, and click again when it's back. Customers see crossed-out colours greyed out on every design of that size and on Custom Designs, and can't order them. Changes save straight away.
